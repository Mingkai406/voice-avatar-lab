Event-based mode keeps phrases together and distinguishes brief transitions, restarts and longer retrieval pauses. Edit individual gaps below the Speech timeline.

How this demo works

Your input → local conversation or explicit cue rules → speech plan → system or Qwen3-TTS audio → DH_live mouth animation.

The portrait is a repository sample, not newly generated. Source video supplies its body and much of its head motion. LivePortrait is the separate expression experiment.

Future VOICE integration can replace the dialogue and speech components while keeping the renderer boundary.
