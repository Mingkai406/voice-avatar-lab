# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Welcome contributors with different clinical, technical and design backgrounds.

Do not harass, discriminate, publish private information, impersonate people or post unauthorized patient material. Use synthetic examples in public discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. For sensitive concerns, request a private contact channel without publishing details in an Issue; for security vulnerabilities use private vulnerability reporting. Reports will be handled with discretion, and retaliation is not acceptable.
